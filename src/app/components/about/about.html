About Our Ice Cream Ordering App

The Ice Cream Ordering App is designed to make ordering ice cream simple, fast, and efficient. Customers can browse the menu, customize orders, add items to a cart, and schedule pickup times.

The system also gives admins tools to manage menu items, track quantities, and view customer orders.
